# Pitch email template

Rules baked in: ≤150 words, plain text, one link, every number has a window, one CTA. Replace {brackets}; delete any line that isn't true rather than padding.

```
Subject: {Creator} × {Brand}: {specific content idea}

Hi {FirstName},

I'm {Creator} ({handle}) — {one-line niche + the strongest verified number, e.g. "I make {niche} videos for a {top-geo} audience; my {format} series averages {avg} views with {er}% engagement (last 30 days)"}.

Specific idea for {Brand}: {1–2 sentences — a concrete video concept featuring their product/venue, referencing a proven format from top_content}.

Recent proof: "{top video title}" reached {views} views, {er}% engagement ({window} window).

Rate card and full media kit attached — happy to tailor deliverables (multi-platform bundle available).

Worth a chat?
{Name}
{media kit link}
```

## Follow-up bump (one only, 5–7 days later)

```
Subject: Re: {Creator} × {Brand}: {idea}

Hi {FirstName} — floating this back up in case it got buried. The {format} idea still stands; happy to send the media kit if useful. If the timing's wrong, a one-word "later" works too.
```

## Rules of thumb

- No attachments in the first cold email — link the media kit instead.
- Mention ONE number per claim, always with its window.
- Personalize the first line with something specific to the brand (their campaign, their store, their product launch).
- Plain-text style; skip heavy HTML, tracking pixels, and multiple links.
